How to use this page
  1. Read the title and abstract. The numbered tag is the study's number in the methodology figure; "Stages" are the figure's stage numbers.
  2. The 0, 1 or 2 buttons start with the AI's scores. Change any score you disagree with; the total and the rule's suggestion update by themselves.
  3. To share your changes, type your name and click Export CSV. Changes stay in this browser until you export them.

Blue boxes are the AI's words, not the authors'. Check them against the abstract. The full ranking and the stage map are in docs/systematic-review/reading-list.md.
