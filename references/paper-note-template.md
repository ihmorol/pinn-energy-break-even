# Paper note template

This file is a blank template, not a record of a checked paper. Copy it under a descriptive paper filename and replace each unrecorded field with a checked value or an explicit access limit.

## Source

- Title: not recorded
- Authors: not recorded
- Year and publication or preprint version: not recorded
- DOI or source URL: not recorded
- Date checked: not recorded
- Reader: not recorded
- Access: not recorded; state full text, abstract only, or secondary account
- Local file or approved storage location and SHA-256, if downloaded: not recorded

## Question and setting

State the paper's question, PDEs, hardware, methods, and workload. Note whether it tests a single solve or new parameter queries. Include the accuracy criterion and whether it measures energy, runtime, or another cost.

## Claims we may use

For each claim, record the exact supporting location, a short quotation or faithful summary, and its limits. Keep the authors' result separate from our interpretation. Do not fill a citation gap with a plausible statement.

## Relevance to this project

Explain what the source changes about the fixed research question or method. A related paper does not justify expanding our scope without approval.

## Check status and gaps

State what is confirmed, what is unresolved, and what needs a later check. Include corrections or conflicts with other sources. Do not mark a claim as supported if the cited passage was not read.
