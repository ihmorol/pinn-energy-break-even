## Question and setting

Position paper arguing that computational efficiency should be an evaluation criterion alongside accuracy. Reports that deep learning computation doubled every few months (an estimated 300,000x increase from 2012 to 2018), proposes reporting the cost ("price tag") of developing, training, and running models, and contrasts cost-chasing "Red AI" with efficiency-conscious "Green AI".

## Claims we may use

- Making efficiency a standard evaluation criterion is the field-level remedy to rising computation. Position claim of the paper (abstract).
- Reporting cost alongside accuracy is feasible and was not standard practice when the paper was written. Abstract framing.

## Relevance to this project

This is the framing citation for why a Green Computing course project should report energy at matched accuracy rather than report accuracy alone. Our crossover plot and comparison table are a concrete instance of its efficiency-as-criterion proposal applied to PDE solving.
